Download Glance How it works

Free and open source. One download for both Classic and Forever.

Everything in a fight, where your eyes already are

Glance replaces the player and target frames with two compact groups of bars that meet in the middle of the screen. Every part has a checkbox, and help mode explains anything you hover.

See every part explained

Install

  1. Download the latest Glance-<version>.zip from Releases.
  2. Unzip it into the Interface/AddOns/ folder of the game you play (for Classic that's World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Interface/AddOns/), so you have AddOns/Glance/Glance.toc. The same zip works in Classic and Forever.
  3. Restart the game, or type /reload.

That's it

There's nothing to set up. Log in and Glance is already in place, just below your character in the middle of the screen, with Blizzard's frames tucked away. Help mode starts on, so you can hover any part to see what it means. When you're ready to make it yours, you can move and resize the bars or switch on minimal mode, but you never have to.
